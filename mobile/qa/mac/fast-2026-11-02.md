## How to read the screen-open row

taps-analyze.mjs times "drawn" as the last frame the app's UI thread drew after the touch. On this build Beranda's
and Tugas' wave keep drawing (also when another tab or a pushed screen covers them), so almost every step is marked
"frames already running before the touch", and "drawn" runs on: to the next tap (~2.5 s) after a step on or back
to Beranda or Tugas, and some hundreds of ms otherwise. So the trace's numbers are upper bounds, and the first-frame
numbers are lower bounds; the screen with all its data lies in between. The desktop's mid "after" column used the
trace's "drawn" too (class sheet 189, Nilai 602), so compare like with like only once the wave stops drawing when
it's hidden (PROGRESS.md: "loops in hidden screens: fix queued").
