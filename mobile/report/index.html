Edunex Plus for Android, round 2

Round 1 put every desktop feature on the phone. Arqila's verdict was that it was cluttered, wordy and joyless. Round 2 rebuilt every screen in v4 (course colours, big numbers, Plus Jakarta Sans, Material 3 motion), cut the words, and fixed what Arqila found broken. It also made the app fast and steady on a phone.

What broke along the way, and why

  • Sheets stayed off-screen with Android's animations off (CI's emulator, and anyone with "Remove animations"). A bug in react-native-screens. Fixed with a patch of 4.30.1 (upstream's #4581, not yet released).
  • Cards invisible on a tab's first visit. Their entrance animation started while the tab was hidden and never ended. They now appear plainly unless the screen is in front.
  • A second-long freeze on every start with saved data. On the phone the engine shares one JS thread with the screens. Reading three of SIX's catalog pages and its curriculum page (about 300 KB of HTML each) held it for up to 1.4 s. They're now parsed in slices with the screens drawing between them.
  • "Semua siap" came and went after the first sign-in. SIX counted as signed out while it was still joining. A system still signing in now counts as coming.
  • Screens kept drawing at rest, up to 120 fps for a wave nobody could see. Loops now run only while their screen is in front and the app is open.
  • The lab machine went down three times. Its old F: disk stalled and its C: filled up. Builds moved to GitHub Actions, and the speed numbers were taken on the Mac.
  • Tests that failed for reasons outside the app. Android sometimes dropped a launch right after the app's data was cleared, and the new one-time question about problem reports covered the bottom of the screen. The flows now retry the launch and answer the question.

What's still weak

  • The JS thread still blocks ~400 ms on a start with saved data, after Beranda shows. It's the engine's own start (loading and checking the saved data, the first alert plan) on Hermes. Finding which needs a profiler on a device.
  • Nilai and Rencana open in 550–600 ms on mid, over 500. Weak's readings are upper bounds, because the wave kept the screen busy.
  • Tugas scrolled 20% janky on weak. The wave now slides as a cached texture. That needs re-measuring.
  • Mid's numbers for today are void. The Mac ran out of memory while measuring. Retake them on the lab machine.
  • The one-time question about problem reports (from main) is a paragraph and four buttons over a third of a tab, against the text budget. It's main's design, so it's left as it is for Arqila to decide.
  • For about a second before the first read's data, Beranda's placeholder looks like a grey blob.
  • The Kumpulkan sheet's second button is cut off at the bottom in CI's pictures. The sheet already leaves room for the phone's bar, so finding why needs a device.
  • Polish the judge still wants. Stack screens use two title faces (Plus Jakarta on Perlu kamu urus and Lisensi, Roboto on the rest). A few one-line metas are cut mid-word where a shorter line would read better. The Jadwal menu's status bar went blank in light in the last pictures.
  • Signing in with a real ITB account hasn't been tried. Everything after the sign-in page works with the demo's made-up one.

Run it

  1. The demo, no account needed. adb install edunex-plus-demo-release.apk, then open Edunex Plus Demo. A made-up student on a pretend Edunex and SIX, on the recorded Thursday (8 Oct 2026).
  2. The real app. adb install edunex-plus-release.apk, open Edunex Plus and sign in on ITB's own page.

Both APKs come from the Mobile workflow on branch mobile.

How this was made

Made with Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code (run from T3 Code), as the lead on one machine. Helpers in separate Claude Code sessions (Claude Opus 5.5) on a Mac and a Windows desktop built pieces on their own branches, and the lead reviewed and merged each. The judge ran four passes over every screen, light and dark. Pass 1 was Codex (gpt-6-luna), with 27 of 37 at 8+. Passes 2 to 4 were Claude Opus 5.5, going from 14 to 34 of 37 in light and from 16 to 35 in dark.
