Get started

Three steps, about two minutes.

Free for every ITB student, on macOS, Windows and Linux.

  1. 1

    Download the file for your computer

    From the newest release, under Assets.

    Mac (Apple silicon or Intel)Windows 10 or 11Ubuntu, Debian, other Linux
  2. 2

    Open it

    The app isn't signed yet, so macOS and Windows warn you the first time. The install guide shows the one step past that.

  3. 3

    Sign in with your INA account

    Once, on ITB's real Microsoft page. Edunex and SIX both connect from that one sign-in.

Your data stays on your computer.

  • Your password never reaches the app. Only Microsoft sees it.
  • Locked in your keychain. What it reads is saved only on this computer.
  • No analytics, no tracking. "Sign out & remove data" in Settings deletes everything it saved.
